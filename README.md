### Hi, I'm Pablo

I'm in my fourth year of Mathematical Engineering at Universidad Complutense de Madrid, specializing in the Economics and Mathematics track. I like using mathematics to answer concrete questions about how things work in the real world.

**Featured project:** [Why is the milk always at the back of the supermarket?](https://github.com/pabloalcalab/supermarket-graph-theory). I surveyed 100 shoppers, laser-measured a real store and used graph theory and exact route optimization in Python to test whether the most-bought products are placed far from the entrance.

**Tools:** MATLAB, R, Python, Excel.
**Currently learning:** pandas, NetworkX and matplotlib, which I used for the project above.
